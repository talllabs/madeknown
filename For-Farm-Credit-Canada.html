Issued RFP – St. Thomas, U.S. Virgin Islands

VIPA RFP: Video Production of Aviation and Marine Facilities

The Virgin Islands Port Authority (VIPA) is seeking qualified video production professionals or firms to produce a series of educational and promotional videos highlighting the critical role of its airports and seaports in supporting commerce, employment, travel, and the overall economy of the U.S. Virgin Islands.

RFP Number VIPA-RFP-2026-006
Status Issued
Issue Date January 5, 2026
Proposal Deadline February 9, 2026 – 5:00 PM (AST)
Question Cut-Off Date January 15, 2026 – 8:00 AM (AST)
Location St. Thomas, U.S. Virgin Islands

Project Overview

VIPA requires on-location filming at aviation and marine facilities to capture real-world operations, including passenger activity, cargo handling, ferry operations, and terminal environments. The final videos will be used across television, digital platforms, social media, websites, and community presentations.

Key Requirements

  • On-location filming at VIPA airports and seaports is required.
  • Capture busy operational activities such as cargo transshipment, ferry loading, and traveler movement.
  • VIPA will provide limited aerial footage; new drone footage is not required.
  • Contractor must source historical footage and photographs as needed.
  • Licensing fees for archival content must not be included in the cost proposal.
  • Access to secured areas will be coordinated through VIPA’s Public Relations Office.
