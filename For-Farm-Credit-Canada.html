Made Known Pictures is pleased to submit this proposal in response to RFP No. PS25-RN-097 for Video Production Services in support of FCC’s Let’s Grow Canada / On sort du rang initiative.

We specialize in creating clear, human, and story-driven video content for organizations operating in complex, real-world industries. FCC’s mandate—supporting Canadian agriculture and food through knowledge, relationships, and expertise—aligns directly with the type of storytelling we deliver: authentic, field-based, and built to connect with real audiences.

We understand this opportunity is not a one-time project, but a long-term partnership supported through Statements of Work. Our model is designed specifically for this structure. Made Known operates with a scalable, Canada-wide production approach—combining centralized creative direction with regional crews—to deliver consistent quality, reduce travel costs, and respond quickly to evolving needs.

  • A nimble production model capable of handling multiple assignments simultaneously
  • Strong experience capturing real people and real environments with authenticity
  • A modular content approach that maximizes output from every shoot
  • Reliable turnaround times, including fast-turn edits when required

Our goal is to be an easy, dependable partner for FCC—one that can take a brief, execute efficiently, and deliver content that is both practical and impactful across multiple channels.
