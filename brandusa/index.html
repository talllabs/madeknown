1
B2B Destination + Human Story | Tourism Papua New Guinea

Two Stories, One Assignment, New York

Tourism Papua New Guinea brought us to New York to capture a polished media lunch designed to place the destination in front of travel media, advisors, and decision-makers. We delivered that assignment with energy, professionalism, and an eye for what matters in a trade setting.

But while filming, we noticed something more human unfolding. Fabian Paino, visiting New York for the very first time, was experiencing the city with wonder, curiosity, and joy. We pitched the client on the spot: let us tell that story too. They said yes.

The result was two distinct films from one trip — one strategic B2B asset, and one emotional travel story that reminds viewers why people travel in the first place.

2
Lifestyle Commercial + Mini Documentary | Lutheran World Relief

Coffee Is Life — Nicaragua

This began as a commercial about coffee — warmth, ritual, aroma, the feeling of the first sip in the morning. But instead of building it with actors and staged scenes, we traveled to Nicaragua and met the families whose lives revolve around growing it.

We listened to their voices, watched their hands at work, and built the piece from what was already true. Their words became the script. Their presence became the performance.

The final film feels polished and premium, yet deeply human. It carries the beauty of advertising with the honesty of documentary.

Why We Selected This Showcases our work with non actors that feels cinematic without losing authenticity using their own words for the scripting.
3
Documentary / Social Impact in Ecuador | Andean Health & Development

Ecuador in Motion

The brief was to connect with donors. But instead of making a conventional appeal, we believed people would respond more deeply to truth, place, and human connection.

We traveled into the Andes and followed both the people doing the work and the people whose lives were changed by it. The mountains, roads, faces, and quiet moments became as important as the message itself.

The result is a film with emotional gravity — one that moves people because it respects the audience enough to tell a real story.

Mini Documentary | Lutheran World Relief

A Young Potato Farmer Named Daisy

Daisy lives in Peru, surrounded by a landscape many productions would treat as scenery. We treated it as part of her identity.

This is a patient, quiet portrait of Daisy and the Andes.

Sometimes the most powerful stories are the smallest ones, told with care.

Why We Selected This We love how intimate storytelling can create a deep emotional connection to a destination.
