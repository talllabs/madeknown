About Us

Made Known Pictures

Made Known is comprised of filmmakers, designers, and content strategists who love storytelling. We make commercials, mini-docs, founder profiles, explainer videos, and branded content.

We love helping people tell their unique and personal stories, extending their reach through the power of video. Made Known Pictures was founded in 2007 by Scott Blanding and Brad LaBriola with the mission to produce hidden stories that should be made known to a wider audience. They have made commercials, mini-documentaries and promotional films for apparel companies, cooking apps, and non-profits supporting coffee farmers in Kenya.

SB
DP, Writer, Director

Scott Blanding

A life-long film lover, Scott gradually combined his concern for social justice with his passion for film and communication. Most notably was Women in War Zones (Best Documentary, 2009 Philadelphia Film Festival and Cinefest), a documentary he shot and directed with Brad about two victims of sexual violence at Panzi Hospital in the DRC.

After growing up in Roanoke, VA, Scott pursued filmmaking in Nicaragua, the Czech Republic and the USA, graduating from Temple University in Philadelphia, PA. He now lives in LA, serves as a reservist in the Marines, and continues working with Women In War Zones International and Made Known Pictures.

BL
Producer, Writer, Editor

Brad LaBriola

Brad's love for storytelling started with books. With the mentorship of Robert Pledge, CEO of Contact Press Images and featured in the Academy Award winning Born Into Brothels, Brad has produced films on four continents in five languages.

From 2010–2012, Brad earned his MFA at the UCLA Producers Program, with mentorship by Oscar-winning producers Peter Guber, David Hoberman, Bobby Moresco, and Tom Nunan. His commitment is to produce deeply-felt stories that bring meaning to the confusion of our lives and renew a sense of wonder in humanity.

"One of the most meaningful things I've done is helping others tell their story."
