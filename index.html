What We Offer

Development

You have a story — whether the story of your organization or the personal story of someone you serve. It is unique, unlike any other story. With mentorship from academy award winners and nominees, we have developed dozens of concepts.

About Us

Made Known Pictures

Made Known is comprised of filmmakers, designers, and content strategists who love storytelling.

We make commercials, mini-docs, founder profiles, explainer videos, and branded content. We love helping people tell their unique and personal stories, extending their reach through the power of video. Made Known Pictures was founded in 2007 by Scott Blanding and Brad LaBriola with the mission to produce hidden stories that should be made known to a wider audience.

DP, Writer, Director

Scott Blanding

A life-long film lover, Scott gradually combined his concern for social justice with his passion for film and communication. Most notably was Women in War Zones (Best Documentary, 2009 Philadelphia Film Festival and Cinefest), a documentary he shot and directed about two victims of sexual violence at Panzi Hospital in the DRC.

After growing up in Roanoke, VA, Scott pursued filmmaking in Nicaragua, the Czech Republic and the USA, graduating from Temple University. He now lives in LA, serves as a reservist in the Marines, and continues working with Women In War Zones International.

Producer, Writer, Editor

Brad LaBriola

With the mentorship of Robert Pledge, CEO of Contact Press Images (featured in the Academy Award winning Born Into Brothels), Brad has produced films on four continents in five languages. From 2010–2012 he earned his MFA at the UCLA Producers Program under Oscar-winning mentors Peter Guber, Bobby Moresco, and Tom Nunan.
