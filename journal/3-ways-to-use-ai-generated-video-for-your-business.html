3 Simple Ways to Use AI-Generated Video for Your Business

Dec 13, 2024  ·  By Made Known

It's not a stretch to say that AI-generated video is rapidly changing the landscape of content creation. A few years ago, producing a polished corporate video required a full crew, a studio, and a significant budget. Today, AI tools allow businesses to produce quality video content faster and at a fraction of the cost — without sacrificing the message.

But here's the thing: AI video on its own is just a tool. Like any tool, what matters is how you use it. Below are three practical, real-world ways businesses are using AI-generated video right now — and how you can too.

01

Boost Your Marketing Content

One of the highest-leverage uses of AI video is in marketing. Whether you're running paid social ads, updating your website, or filling your content calendar, AI lets you produce more — without proportionally increasing your costs.

Think about the traditional content cycle: script → shoot → edit → approve → publish. For a single 30-second ad, that cycle might take weeks and thousands of dollars. AI-assisted video can compress that down to days — or even hours — for certain content types.

Here's where it makes the most sense:

  • Product explainers and demos — AI avatars or narrated slide videos can walk customers through a product without a camera crew.
  • Social media content — Short-form video for Instagram Reels, TikTok, or YouTube Shorts can be generated at volume, then A/B tested for performance.
  • Localized versions — Once you have a base video, AI can generate translated voiceovers or on-screen text for different markets, dramatically expanding your reach.

The key is to pair AI efficiency with authentic storytelling. Let AI handle the volume work, and reserve your human (or professional) production budget for the flagship content that truly represents your brand.

02

Host Virtual Events with AI Speakers

Virtual events exploded during the pandemic and never really went away — because they work. They eliminate travel costs, open your audience to anyone with an internet connection, and can be recorded for evergreen content use.

Now layer in AI: with tools like HeyGen, Synthesia, or ElevenLabs + video synthesis, you can create AI-generated speaker presentations that look and sound surprisingly real. This opens up possibilities that weren't available before:

  • Founders who can't travel can still "appear" at events in multiple cities simultaneously.
  • Training and onboarding videos can be refreshed quickly when policies change — no need to reshoot.
  • Thought leadership content can be generated from an existing interview transcript and repurposed across formats.

One real-world example: a nonprofit we worked with used an AI-generated version of their executive director to open a virtual donor gala. The director was traveling internationally and couldn't attend. Their AI "double" gave a personalized 3-minute welcome address — complete with the director's voice and likeness — and donors loved it.

Note: Always be transparent with your audience when AI is involved. Authenticity builds trust — and trust is the foundation of any lasting relationship with your customers or donors.

03

Personalize Customer and Donor Videos at Scale

This is the one that genuinely changes the game.

Personalization has always been a proven driver of conversions — whether you're selling a product or cultivating a donor relationship. But until recently, true video personalization was only accessible to enterprise brands with massive budgets.

AI changes that. Tools now exist that let you generate thousands of unique video messages — each one addressed to a specific person, referencing their name, their history with your brand, or their specific interests — at near-zero marginal cost per video.

Here's what that looks like in practice:

  • E-commerce brands sending personalized "thank you" videos post-purchase, with the customer's name and the exact products they bought.
  • Nonprofits sending year-end donor recaps: "Thanks to you, [Name], we served 1,200 families this year" — with footage that matches their donation level or area of interest.
  • B2B sales teams using AI-generated personalized video outreach to cut through inbox noise and dramatically improve response rates.

When people feel seen — when a message is clearly meant for them and not just blasted at them — they respond. That's not new psychology. What's new is that AI finally makes it accessible to businesses of all sizes.

The Bottom Line

AI-generated video isn't about replacing human creativity. It's about removing the barriers — time, cost, logistics — that prevent great stories from being told at the scale they deserve.

The businesses that will win over the next five years are those that learn to combine the efficiency of AI with the irreplaceable power of authentic human storytelling. One without the other is incomplete.
